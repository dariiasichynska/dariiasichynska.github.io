Snap & List

From a photo of a piece of clothing to a finished German sales listing. The AI analyses and writes; a human checks and publishes. The complete workflow is built twice: in Make.com and in n8n.

My role
Concept, implementation and operation, on my own
Tools
Make.com, n8n, Google Gemini API (Claude for comparison), Dropbox, Gmail, Airtable, Telegram, BPMN, iOS Shortcuts
Status
In use for my own sales

Starting point

Every listing was manual work: look through the photos, determine the features, write a text in German. The quality of the descriptions varied, and the effort grew with every item.

  • Efficiency: manual work only for the final check.
  • Quality: consistent, complete descriptions for the German-speaking market.
  • Budget: no infrastructure costs, running on free tiers.
  • Control: no automatic publishing; the decision stays with a human.

Process

  1. The photos of each item land as a separate package in cloud storage.
  2. On a schedule, the process picks up new packages and sends all photos of one item to the AI model in a single request.
  3. The AI determines category, condition, material, colour, size and brand and writes the title and description.
  4. The text arrives in my inbox for review: in Make.com as an e-mail to me, in n8n as a Gmail draft. The Make.com version also registers the item in Airtable.
  5. The processed package is archived automatically.

Decisions

Process analysisSplit the sales process into single steps, defined the automation potential and the human checkpoints, modelled it in BPMN.
ArchitectureScheduled batch processing instead of reacting to every single file: one item, one package, one AI request, one draft. This prevents duplicates and saves resources.
Scarce resourcesCalculated usage for the free plan (1,000 operations, 2 active scenarios per month) at about 10 items with 100 photos. When the data volume was no longer enough, I moved image compression into the capture step.
Multimodal AIAll views of an item in one request. Structured JSON output, so the data flows into e-mail and database without rework.
Choice of providerCompared Google Gemini and Anthropic Claude by cost, access conditions and quality. Where standard connectors did not support the data format, connected directly via the REST API.
PromptA German system prompt with clear rules: structure of the listing, a note on private sale, no personal data such as name labels. Main and fallback model chosen by quality of results.
ResilienceRetries on API errors, faulty files are skipped, error notifications, fallback model under overload.
Platform switchMoved the solution from Make.com to n8n and compared both approaches.
DocumentationA BPMN diagram and an overview of obstacles and solutions.

Result

  • A continuous process from the photo to the finished listing draft, without writing it myself.
  • No infrastructure costs: at the current volume everything stays on free tiers.
  • Consistent, complete descriptions thanks to clear rules for the AI.
  • The automation prepares, I decide.

The same logic can be applied to other products, platforms and business processes with image and document processing.

    My approach

    From analysis to everyday use.

    1. A process takes too much time
    2. Capture the current process
      Example: Olympiade-Training, requirements settled through multiple-choice questions
    3. Model it in BPMN
      Example: Snap & List, process drawn in BPMN first
    4. AI needed?
      yes: AI modelno: fixed rules
      Example: Agentic workflow, addresses by rule instead of by AI
    5. Build and test in Make or n8n
      Example: Olympiade-Training, tested on a tablet from 2014
    6. Human in the loop: a person checks the result
      Example: Snap & List, a draft instead of automatic publishing
    7. In use, improved continuously
